# Roadmap audit and suggested adjustments

Written 30 Oct 2026 against `06-stages-roadmap.md`. These are suggestions for Zac to accept or reject, not decisions. Figures marked "typical" are general industry ranges, not quotes.

## The one change that matters most

**Try to run the trial off site, as a supplier, before SightShield is an approved contractor.** The roadmap puts the trial (Stage 3) after contractor onboarding (Stage 2), which is three to four months of waiting on other people, over Christmas. But the test assemblies are removable parts. If Rhani can get them sent to Moree, Zac films them in the shop, they go back and the site's own maintenance crew fits them in the normal rotation. SightShield then supplies a product; it does not do work on site. Supplier onboarding is usually far lighter than contractor onboarding, and no induction, medical or site access is needed for the trial.

That turns the plan from a chain into two parallel tracks:

- Track A (product): test assemblies, film, protocol, data. Can start the week the assemblies arrive.
- Track B (access): entity, insurance, prequal, inductions, MOC. Runs in the background and is needed for the production rollout, not the trial.

Ask Rhani this first tomorrow. If the answer is no, the roadmap stands as written.

## Stage 2: contractor onboarding (needed for rollout, not the trial if the off-site route works)

- **Do not pay for a prequal platform until the site names it.** Each one charges an annual fee (typically several hundred to over a thousand dollars) and sites are specific about which they accept.
- **Request SDS now, at no cost.** The film and adhesive SDS come from the manufacturer. Ask the AutoTriz dealer this week. They take time to arrive and every portal asks for them. For the trial, use an existing cleaner with a published SDS and keep the new cleaner as its own R&D arm.
- **Inductions and medicals are the hidden long pole.** If the site is in Queensland, Standard 11 and the coal board medical typically cost in the low thousands combined and need booking weeks ahead. Confirm with Rhani, then book once Stage 0 is done, not after prequal is approved.
- **Christmas.** Stage 2 runs Dec to Feb. Mine contractor management teams slow down from mid-December to late January. Expect this stage to slip a month unless the paperwork is submitted before 10 December.
- **MOC.** Start the fitment approval conversation in the Rhani call (question 5). Offer to supply the trial data as the evidence. The roadmap already says this is the long pole; it is also the one Zac cannot speed up by working harder, so it has to start first.

## Stage 3: trial and cleaner R&D

- **Controls.** The trial needs untreated mirrors on the same machines, cleaned the same way, or the result is an opinion. Ask the site to tag two or three control assemblies.
- **Measure, do not just photograph.** A handheld gloss and haze meter (typical cost low thousands) gives a number for optical clarity at each inspection. It is an R&D capital item and it is what a maintenance superintendent will believe over a photo. Pair it with a fixed photo jig and the same lighting each time.
- **Keep the variables apart.** Two films plus a new cleaner on the same assemblies means nothing can be attributed. Suggested arms: film A with the site's current cleaner, film B with the site's current cleaner, and the new cleaner on film A only. The cleaner still gets proven, separately.
- **Second film brand.** Pick it now so it can be ordered with the first roll. Candidates exist in the windscreen protection film market from other PPF makers; Zac knows this market better than any document does. The requirement is a windscreen-grade film with a self-healing top coat and a published warranty.
- **R&D records from day one.** Tania's advice stands. Write the hypothesis before the trial ("film X keeps clarity above Y after Z cleaning cycles under coal dust"), log each inspection, keep timesheets. Registration of R&D activities with AusIndustry has a deadline after year end, so the July timing Tania gave is the start of that process, not the end. Confirm the minimum spend threshold with her before assuming a claim is worthwhile in year one.

## Stage 4: decide and productise

- **Pricing shape.** The two-year re-film cycle is the business. Price per mirror with a per-visit mobilisation fee for site work, and quote a per-machine-per-year figure alongside, because that is the number the site compares with its replacement spend. Decide after the trial, model it now from the roll economics.
- **Warranty.** Two years is the manufacturer's optical clarity figure, not SightShield's tested figure. Write the warranty to what the trial shows, with normal cleaning defined (which cleaner, how often) so the warranty and the cleaner sell each other.
- **Brand, done in the background.** Zac has said branding is chipped away at and never takes time from anything important. Agreed, with one exception: a free IP Australia trade mark search on "SightShield" and "Sight Shield" in the relevant classes should happen in Stage 1, not Stage 4. If the name is taken, finding out after insurance certificates, prequal profiles and safety documents carry it means redoing all of them. Fifteen minutes now, filing later.
- **Domain.** Check availability at the same time as the trade mark search, for the same reason.

## Stage 5: build-out and handover

- **Check whether ISO certification is required or just preferred.** Certification to 9001 and 45001 for a one-person company is a real cost (typically five figures over the first cycle, plus the time) and most Tier 1 sites accept an ISO-aligned safety management system plus prequal from small contractors. Ask the site in Stage 2 what their actual threshold is. Build ISO-aligned regardless, as the roadmap says; certify only if a contract depends on it.
- **The person.** Recruiting or training an installer takes longer than the roadmap allows if it starts in October 2027. Write the role description in Stage 4 and start looking as soon as the first contract is signed. A subcontract installer paid per site is a lower-risk first step than an employee.
- **Systems.** Quoting, job records and warranty tracking can be a small Supabase and Netlify app like the In Line Signs job sheets, but not until there is a second site. For the first site a spreadsheet and the R&D diary are enough.
