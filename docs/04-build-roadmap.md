# Build roadmap

What has to exist for Sight Shield to operate, in the order the dependencies allow. This is the "everything needs building" list. It is drawn from the staged plan in the Stage 0 doc, the accountant agenda and what In Line Signs already has that Sight Shield does not. It is a proposal for Zac to edit, not a decision.

## A. Entity and money (Stage 1, under way)

1. ASIC registration, ACN, ABN, GST, TFN. Beveridge Carey, in progress.
2. Bank account and card.
3. Xero file with a chart of accounts that separates trial, R&D expense, R&D capital, national travel and international travel. Connect to Claude.
4. Payroll for Zac: Single Touch Payroll, super, PAYG withholding.
5. Workers comp through icare.
6. Public liability and product liability through PSC (Kathie Barratt).
7. Funds contributed record: how much Zac put in and when.
8. R&D diary from day one: hypotheses, test plan, photos, timesheets.

## B. Site access (Stages 0 and 2)

1. Stage 0 answers from Rhani, in writing, into the requirements table.
2. Safety management system from On Point Safety: safety management plan, SWMS for film application on mobile plant, risk assessments, SDS for film and cleaner, drug and alcohol policy, fatigue policy.
3. Prequalification registration on whatever platform the site names.
4. Inductions, medicals and fitness-for-work for Zac.
5. Management-of-change or fitment approval submission with the evidence the site asks for.
6. Longer term: ISO certification (the goal stated on 4 Oct).

## C. Product (Stage 3)

1. Supply agreement or account with the AutoTriz dealer for windscreen film.
2. Mirror assembly templates: measure the test assemblies, cut files on the plotter, paired kits.
3. Trial protocol based on the proposed success criteria, with inspection dates and photo log.
4. Trial report format that answers the site's yes or no.
5. Cleaner: specification and sourcing, then SDS.
6. Later: own-brand film and aftercare through the Malaysian factory contact.

## D. Commercial (Stage 4)

1. Start-up cost list and a per-vehicle cost model from the roll economics (about 80 vehicles per $2,000 roll, about $260 plus per vehicle supply and install).
2. Pricing in the format the site buys in (tender, preferred supplier or purchase order).
3. Quote, SWMS and warranty document templates in the Sight Shield brand.
4. Two-year warranty terms in writing.
5. Re-film schedule so each site is booked again at two years.

## E. Identity and presence

1. Final logo and a short brand reference (colours, type, lockups) from the canvas.
2. Name check on IP Australia, trade mark decision.
3. Domain, email address and phone number of its own, or a documented decision to stay inside Mackenzie & Brown's tenant for now.
4. One-page website or capability statement for prequalification portals.
5. Hard hat decal, vehicle door, film-edge label from the Applications board.

## F. Systems on the Claude side

1. Sight Shield working memory (this repo's `CLAUDE.md`, grown into a Brand Brain equivalent).
2. SharePoint workspace BUSINESS FILES/SightShield/CLAUDE WORKSPACE populated with the same numbered-build layout In Line Signs uses.
3. Supabase project and Netlify site when there is an app or website to host.
4. Skills worth building once the business has rhythm: trial inspection logger, site job sheet and photo log, warranty and re-film reminder, R&D diary capture from Pocket, quote template filler.
5. Scheduled tasks: weekly chase list for Stage 0 and 1 items while setup is in progress.

## G. Handover readiness (the reason it is being built this way)

1. SOPs for measure, cut, install, inspect and warranty.
2. A manager role description and the profit share or equity mechanism, decided with Tania before it is offered.
3. Everything in Sight Shield's own systems so none of it depends on In Line Signs access.
