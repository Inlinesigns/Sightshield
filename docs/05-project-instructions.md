# Claude project instructions (as pasted by Zac, 30 Oct 2026)

Copied from the Sight Shield project in claude.ai so the repo and the project say the same thing. If the two drift, the newer one wins and Claude should flag it.

## What this is

A new, standalone company Zac is building from scratch, separate from In Line Signs, with its own entity, insurances and systems, to supply and install optically clear impact and scratch protection film on mirrors and glass of mining machinery. Long term it is to be run by someone else. Zac's job now is to build it properly so it can be handed over.

## The product

Glass-grade protection film (PPF engineered for glass): disperses impact, self-healing top coat resists swirls from repeated cleaning, holds shattered glass together so sharp contaminants stay out of the work area. Launch media is AutoTriz windscreen film; move to purpose-made proprietary media once proven. A bespoke mine-site-safe cleaner will be developed alongside. Target warranty: 2 years.

## The opportunity

A Tier 1 mining contact reports $3M+ spent over three years on mirror replacements at a single site, driven by cleaning up to six times daily (swirls that impair vision) and light impacts from coal dust and debris. Part of that spend is unavoidable full-assembly replacement from hits; the addressable part is cracked and marred glass.

## Where it's at

The contact has organised test mirror assemblies for a trial. Blocker: the trial can't start on site until the company exists and is accepted as a site contractor. So the immediate goal is the minimum build that gets contractor onboarding done, then the trial, then the full build-out driven by what the trial shows.

## Timeline

Fully operational and certified by around mid-2028 (rest of FY25/26 plus FY26/27). Rough shape: company and compliance scaffolding first, trial and cleaner R&D next, brand and name, then proprietary media and full deployment.

## Workstreams

- Entity setup: Pty Ltd, ABN, bank, insurances (public liability, product liability, workers comp), accountant
- Site readiness: contractor prequalification (Avetta/Cm3 or whatever the site uses), inductions, insurance minimums, management-of-change and fitment approval path for vision-critical glass
- Compliance system: SOPs, SWMS, SDS for film, adhesives and cleaner, built ISO-aligned from day one with certification as the end state
- Product: trial on test assemblies, durability under 6x daily cleaning and coal dust, second film brand tested alongside, cleaner formulation, warranty terms
- Brand: name, identity, registration
- Commercial: pilot pricing, per-mirror and per-site models, warranty costing, first contract
